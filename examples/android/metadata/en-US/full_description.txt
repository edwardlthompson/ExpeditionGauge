ExpeditionGauge is an offline-first automotive HUD for track days and off-road exploration.

Core v1 features:
• Real-time attitude G-meter, speed, heading, and drift angle (β)
• Optional WitMotion BLE IMU, OBD-II, BLE TPMS, and external Bluetooth GPS
• Session recording with CSV/JSON/GPX export
• Playback with drift-colored route visualization

Pure FOSS (MIT). No Google Play Services, Firebase, or closed telemetry.
All optional hardware features default off — phone-only mode works out of the box.
